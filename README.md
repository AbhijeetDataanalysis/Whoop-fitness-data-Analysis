This interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard delivers end-to-end health and performance analytics modeled around WHOOP fitness trackers. Designed to process data across 100K users, the report translates raw biometric measurements into actionable insights on physical strain, sleep quality, workout consistency, and cardiovascular intensity.

The summary and recovery modules highlight core health indicators such as daily calorie expenditure, recovery scores, and sleep efficiency. Users can analyze how sleep duration and deep sleep impact recovery across different sport disciplines—including Running, HIIT, CrossFit, and Cycling—while evaluating overall fitness level distribution from beginner to elite tiers.

Additionally, the activity and heart rate analytics track training trends and cardiovascular performance. Visuals map peak strain thresholds, target heart rate zones, and workout completion rates across different age groups and times of day, providing a comprehensive overview of user wellness and operational performance.
